# Prevention of Sexual Harassment  

## Question 1  

### What types of behaviour constitute sexual harassment?

**Physical Harassment**
- Serious physical acts  
- Inappropriate touching  
- Forced physical intimacy  
- Physical blocking or obstruction  

**Verbal Harassment**
- Sexually suggestive remarks  
- Intrusive personal questions  
- Requests or demands of a sexual nature  
- Whistling or suggestive sounds  
- Use of demeaning or offensive language  

**Visual Harassment**
- Online or digital harassment  
- Obscene gestures  
- Circulating explicit content  
- Intimidating or inappropriate staring  

**Quid Pro Quo**
- Threats of negative consequences  
- Promises of special favors or benefits  

---

## Question 2  

### What actions would you take if you experience or witness such behaviour, especially if it occurs repeatedly?

**If I personally experience harassment:**
- Address the situation immediately, if it is safe to do so  
- Record all relevant details  
- Preserve digital or physical evidence  
- Submit a formal complaint to the Internal Committee (IC)  

**If I witness harassment against someone else:**
- Apply the 4 Ds of Bystander Intervention:  
  - Delegate  
  - Document  
  - Delay  
  - Direct  
- Offer support to the affected person  
- Report the incident appropriately  

**In case of repeated incidents:**
- Report the matter to the Internal Committee (IC)  
- Seek further escalation if necessary  
- Use the SHe-Box platform if the IC does not take appropriate action

## Question 1

### Which point(s) were new to you?

One idea that really stood out to me was grouping all questions into a single, well-structured message. Earlier, I used to send my doubts one by one as they came to mind. Now I understand that combining them into one clear message makes it easier for others to respond and saves everyone's time.

## Question 2

### Which area do you think you need to improve on? What are your ideas to make progress in that area?

One area I need to improve is communication, especially when it comes to informing others about delays or blockers. Sometimes I try to solve problems on my own for too long instead of updating the team early.

To improve in this area, I will:

- Inform the team early if I notice any delay or issue.
- Share small and regular updates about my progress.
- Use group communication channels more often instead of relying mainly on private messages.

I believe improving my communication will help me become a more responsible and effective team member.
